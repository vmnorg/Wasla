## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, mission-critical operational cockpit designed specifically for regional agricultural logistics and supply chain networks across the Souss-Massa agricultural basin in Morocco. The product serves commercial farm aggregators, cold-chain freight carriers, cooperative packaging units, and regional produce distributors. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a **Corporate / Modern** framework infused with utilitarian operational clarity. It merges the institutional trustworthiness of institutional supply-chain software with natural biophilic cues: rich emerald canopies, mineral clay from dry alluvial soils, and clean slate architectural whites. The design eliminates decorative friction, enabling operators to digest live fleet telemetry, market price shifts, and bilingual order flows (Latin and Arabic script) effortlessly under demanding field and depot conditions.

## Colors

The palette balances infrastructural confidence with agricultural vitality. The base structure relies on a crisp canvas of `#f8fafc` paired with pure white card surfaces to guarantee high legibility under varying sunlight conditions.

- **Primary (`#059669`)**: Drives core interaction points, primary metrics, active navigation anchors, and logistics confirmation states. Deepened by `emerald-deep` (`#065f46`) for dense structural data tables and lifted by `emerald-light` (`#10b981`) for selection highlights.
- **Secondary (`#c2410c`)**: Earth terracotta denotes physical product classifications, harvest yield analytics, perishable warnings, and regional connection waypoints. Vibrantly highlighted with `terracotta-vibrant` (`#ea580c`) for Darija NLU transcription badges and high-urgency callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#0f172a`)**: Industrial slate provides absolute contrast for top-tier headings, primary wordmarks, telemetry numbers, and high-density typography.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Calibrated slate grey dedicated to metadata, secondary labels, disabled states, and grid dividing rules.
- **Live Status System**: The system utilizes a vivid signal green (`#22c55e`) exclusively for active IoT cold-chain nodes, real-time vehicle GPS tracking, and active transport routes.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid scanning of logistics payloads, truck weights, thermal data, and localized transactions. 

- **Headlines & Metrics**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric weight with friendly, approachable terminal curves, preventing corporate sterility while preserving enterprise authority. High-value data indicators utilize `metric-val` to foreground core KPIs (e.g., metric tons, chill-room temperatures, dirham exchange values).
- **Body & Data Grid**: `Inter` delivers neutral, highly legible rendering across tabular interfaces, manifests, and form fields. Its tall x-height preserves readability on vehicle-mounted tablets and mobile devices in high glare.
- **Micro-labels & Category Captions**: Handled via `label-sm` with forced uppercase tracking (`0.08em`), establishing visual hierarchy above metrics and data values.
- **Bi-directional Support**: When rendering Arabic script (such as Darija NLU transcripts or official Moroccan paperwork), maintain vertical optical centering with an automatic line-height expansion of `1.15x` relative to Latin defaults.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, downscaling to 8 columns on tablets, and collapsing to a 4-column stack on mobile devices. 

- **Grid Geometry**: Maximum desktop canvas constraints anchor at `1440px`. Column gutters remain steady at `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), collapsing to `1rem` (`space-md`) on viewports under `768px`.
- **Canvas Margins**: Outer screen boundaries employ `2rem` (`space-xl`) on desktop surfaces, allowing floating controls to breathe away from the edge of the viewport, reducing to `1rem` (`space-md`) on mobile handheld interfaces.
- **Internal Component Spacing**:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text inline spacing, micro status badges.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner vertical padding, chip interior gaps, compact list groupings.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard card interior padding, metric group vertical spacing, button horizontal padding.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Primary card separation, section division inside sidebars.
  - `space-xl` (32px) and `space-2xl` (48px): Dashboard widget blocks, multi-step logistics form breaks, and page header separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layered plane approach, combining low-contrast border definitions with subtle ambient drop shadows and selective glassmorphism.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Hex `#f8fafc`. Acts as the foundational ground plane for all operational widgets.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Panels)**: Solid white (`#ffffff`) bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). Box shadow is soft and ambient: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Trays & Control Panels)**: Semi-opaque frosted backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a crisp glass border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`). Elevation relies on `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Dispatch Drawers & Toast Notifications)**: High-altitude elements cast a deep, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Directional Gradient Accents**: Waypoint milestones and primary action headers feature subtle 45-degree linear gradients:
  - Emerald Flow: Linear from `#065f46` to `#10b981`.
  - Terracotta Harvest: Linear from `#c2410c` to `#f97316`.

## Shapes

The design uses a rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`), projecting modern approachability while preserving the strict horizontal lines required for data-dense tabular software.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard UI elements including text input fields, table rows, button baselines, and nested metric chips.
- **Medium Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px)**: Standard cards, telemetry summaries, popovers, and floating status pill groups.
- **High Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px)**: Outer boundary cards (`rounded-2xl` equivalent in utility classes), glassmorphic map floating panels, and bottom sheets on mobile views.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for operational tags, status indicators (e.g., cold-chain transit alerts), live telemetry dots, and conversational Darija NLU transcription badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Rich emerald fill (`#059669`) with white typography (`#ffffff`), `0.5rem` border radius, and bold label styling (`label-lg`). Hover shifts background to `#047857` with a subtle elevation boost.
- **Secondary / Action**: Warm terracotta background (`#c2410c`) or outline with white text, utilized strictly for transactions, produce dispatch triggers, and order confirmations.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, dark slate text (`#0f172a`), hairline border (`#e2e8f0`) on resting state; background transitions to `#f1f5f9` on hover.

### Cards & Control Panels
- **Standard Cards**: White background, `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`), bound by `1px solid #e2e8f0` and Level 1 ambient elevation. Padding is fixed at `1.5rem`.
- **Glassmorphic Map Overlays**: Positioned over satellite/route maps with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` background, `12px` blur, and white perimeter highlight. Houses route progress, active speed, and refrigeration sensor data.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Solid white background, `0.5rem` radius, `1px solid #cbd5e1` outline. On focus, applies a `2px` ring in primary emerald (`#059669`) with zero offset. Labels sit consistently above inputs in `label-md` uppercase typography.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes; full round for radios. Accent color uses `#059669`. Unselected borders feature `#94a3b8`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Live Transit Badge**: Pill-shaped container (`9999px`), light green background (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`), text colored in `#065f46`, fronted by a pulsing `8px` dot in `#22c55e`.
- **Darija NLU Chip**: Light terracotta tint (`rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.08)`), text `#c2410c`, pill-shaped with small microphone or wave vector icon, indicating voice-to-text agricultural entry.

### Logistics Metrics Widgets
- Encapsulated containers showcasing a top-stacked category label (`label-sm` in `#64748b`), followed by a primary numeric readout (`metric-val` in `#0f172a`), and bottom-anchored trend badges (e.g., `+4.2% ton yield` in primary emerald).
